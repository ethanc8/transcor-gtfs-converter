# Operators that also have other feeds

Which TDS operators are also published as GTFS elsewhere, found on 2026-11-14 from (a) things the project
already knew about, (b) a sweep of `transitland-atlas` (`feeds/*.dmfr.json`, commit `36ffa3b5` of 2026-11-13, plus
`external-data-for-reference/ntd-gtfs-weblinks`) and (c) downloading every candidate to check it really overlaps.
"Overlap" below means a sample of the feed was compared with the TDS data by agency, trip IDs and route names.
Nothing here is wired into the converter.

## Important: TDS publishes GTFS itself

* `https://maps.tds.ai/download-feed/gtfs-northwestern` returns a GTFS zip generated on request (file name
  `gtfs-northwestern-2026-11-14.zip`, publisher "Transcor Data Services, LLC", contact `gtfs@tds.ai`). It has the
  **same 32 trip IDs** as our conversion, plus things the web data does not give us: `fare_attributes` /
  `fare_rules`, `ticketing_deep_links` / `ticketing_identifiers`, `direction_id`, `stop_code`, `agency_phone`,
  `agency_url`. Its `agency_timezone` is `America/Tijuana` (same offset as Los Angeles, consistent with our verified
  Pacific clock). Atlas lists it as `f-northwestern~stagelines~or`.
* Adirondack Trailways GTFS is also generated by TDS and published to 511NY (see the table). TDS hosts older copies
  at `https://maps.tds.ai/storage/gtfs-feeds/ADT/<hash>/gtfs.zip`; the `NYT` and `PHK` zips in the same location
  are about 500-byte empty stubs.
* The public site has no download links, and we did not guess other `gtfs-<name>` slugs. Ask TDS
  (`gtfs@tds.ai`) which other operators have published feeds.

## Name matches that are different operators

These Atlas feeds look like matches but are not: Transport of Rockland (Rockland County local bus, not Rockland
Coaches), Hazleton Public Transit and County of Lackawanna Transit System (local transit, not the door-to-door
Hazleton Transportation or Expreso Lackawanna), Westchester County Bee-Line System and Glendale Beeline (not
BeeLine Express), Jefferson City Transit, Jefferson Parish and Jefferson County (Alabama) transit (not Jefferson
Lines), and Smile Bus (a Japanese town bus).

## Waukesha County Transit lines 901, 904, 905

Not in any feed above with trips. The Coach USA feed has the three route records and about 70 unreferenced stops in
the area (Waukesha, Pewaukee, Delafield, Oconomowoc and downtown Milwaukee); the same file is at the Trillium URL
(NTD lists it for the City of Waukesha). TDS has no such agency.

## Notes

* Downloads were made to a scratch directory only; nothing was added to the repository.
* The three Trillium operator feeds (Peter Pan, Indian Trails, Michigan Flyer) and Virginia Breeze are out of date,
  so TDS is the current source for those operators.
* Possible uses: Northwestern and Adirondack GTFS straight from TDS (fares, ticketing links), the Coach USA feed
  for its 8 operators, the WSDOT feed for the Apple, Gold and Grape lines, and the official/fixed Amtrak and
  FlixBus/Greyhound feeds as planned.
